Last updated: November 27, 2026.

   game for Reddit, provided as-is for entertainment. There are no purchases,
   no ads, and no prizes run by the developer.
2. **Fair play.** Each day has one board. Taps are resolved server-side; do not

   scores. Abuse may lead to removal from leaderboards.
3. **Your data.** Game progress, streaks, and leaderboard entries are stored

4. **Changes.** The developer may update, suspend, or discontinue the game at
   any time.
5. **Reddit's rules.** Reddit's Terms of Use, User Agreement, and Content

6. **Contact.** Open an issue at
