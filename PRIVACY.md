# WHAT CHANGED — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026.

WHAT CHANGED stores only what it needs to run the game, in Reddit's Devvit
Redis storage, keyed to your Reddit username:

- daily game progress (differences found, time, pings, misses);
- streaks and snoozes;
- per-subreddit leaderboard entries and daily statistics;
- hint and share usage.

The game does not collect your email address, password, location, or any data
outside Reddit. It does not sell data or share data with advertisers or other
third parties, and it makes no calls to external services. Scene data is
retained on a rolling basis (about 21 days). If you stop playing, your stored
game state simply stops updating.
